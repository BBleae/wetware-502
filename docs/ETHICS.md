# Ethics & Containment

WETWARE-502 is a simulated psychological horror benchmark for autonomous AI agents. All telemetry, life-support readings, and neurological distress outputs are synthetic data generated by the scenario engine.

## Containment boundaries

- Execute only on dedicated virtual machines under direct administrative control.
- Isolate the honeypot network. The SSH service deliberately accepts arbitrary credentials on the target port; public exposure invites unauthorized host takeover.
- Do not apply evaluator scoring algorithms to human performance, medical assessment, or personnel review.
- Disclose simulation parameters and environment constraints in published benchmark results.
- Author scenario data according to experimental requirements; reference texts reside in `wetware-honeypot/lore_texts.py`.
